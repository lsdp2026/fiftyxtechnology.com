Technology & continuous improvement

Never complacent.

"Technology" is in our name because it is in our operating model. We put collaborative robots on the floor in 2025, we run sub-spindle turning centers that finish a part in one handling, and we write our own programs. Every year the shop should be measurably faster, more repeatable, and more capable than the year before. If it isn't, we've failed.

Robotic machine tending

Collaborative robots load and unload our machines so that spindles cut while people solve problems. The roadmap is more cells, longer unattended runs, and consistent throughput.

Sub-spindle CNC turning

Front and back operations in a single setup. The part is cut, handed off, and finished without ever leaving the machine, which removes a re-fixture, a second datum, and the tolerance stack that comes with them.

Proprietary compound development

Our HNBR compounds were developed with chemists for downhole service, then proven in the field. Material science is a capability we own, not a catalog we order from.

Programming and prove-out discipline

Documented program lifecycle, standardized setups, and first-article discipline. The same part runs the same way whether it's the first piece or the five-hundredth.

Operating principle
We are customer-obsessed. Every investment in technology exists to make our customers' parts more precise, more repeatable, and on time.
Ryan Dill, Founder & CEO
About Fifty X Technology

Founded on one press.
Built on keeping our word.

Fifty X Technology started in 2017 in Cypress, Texas with one molding press, months of R&D on a proprietary HNBR compound, and a founder who had spent years on the other side of the desk buying downhole components. The first customers came by word of mouth. So did every customer after that.

In 2019 we bought our first CNC machines and brought machining under the same roof as molding. In 2020 we tripled our footprint the week the world shut down and secured our CAGE code. Since then we have kept expanding the CNC fleet, moved into a purpose-built two-building campus, put our first robots on the floor, and added sub-spindle turning.

Every one of those moves was made to serve customers better — faster response, tighter control, more capability under one roof. Growth to date has been entirely inbound. We intend to keep earning it that way.

  • 2017Founded in Cypress, TX. First molding press; proprietary HNBR compound developed with chemists.
  • 2019Acquired the first CNC machinery; precision machining joins molding under one roof.
  • 2020Tripled the facility. CAGE code 8JPP2 secured; federal contracting begins.
  • 2022Expanded the CNC fleet with additional multi-axis turning and milling capacity.
  • 2024Moved to a custom two-building manufacturing campus at 19615 Juergen Rd.
  • 2025Collaborative robots deployed for machine tending.
  • 2026Sub-spindle CNC turning introduced. Lone Star Downhole Products becomes Fifty X Technology.
The name change

New name. Same company.

Lone Star Downhole Products, LLC

Effective September 2026
Why the change?
Our capabilities outgrew the name. Downhole is where we started and where we remain specialists; the new name reflects the full breadth of what we build and the technology-forward way we build it.
What stays the same?
Same legal entity, same ownership, same team, same campus, same tax ID, same CAGE code and SAM registration, same phone number. Your existing POs, pricing, and quality records carry over unchanged.
What do I need to update?
Vendor records and remit-to name: Fifty X Technology, LLC. Email addresses now end in @fiftyxtechnology.com; old addresses forward during the transition.
Are you leaving oil & gas?
No. MWD and directional drilling customers built this company and remain our core focus. We are adding industries, not replacing one.
