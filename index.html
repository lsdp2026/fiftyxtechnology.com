Capabilities

More capabilities.
Fewer compromises.

What makes Fifty X different is the range of capabilities we bring together under one roof. By combining precision machining, elastomer molding, and fabrication, we provide solutions that would ordinarily require multiple suppliers. The result is less complexity, greater flexibility, and a more effective manufacturing partner.

CNC MACHINING

Precision CNC Machining

Multi-axis turning and milling for complex, tight-tolerance components made from demanding materials. We engineer the complete manufacturing process around the print, from tooling and workholding through machining, inspection, and repeatable production.

  • Non-magnetic stainless, nickel alloys, copper alloys, and tool steels
  • Complex turned and milled components
  • Tight-tolerance threads, bores, sealing surfaces, and critical datums
  • Multi-axis and single-setup machining
  • In-house process engineering, workholding, programming, and prove-out
ENGINEERED ELASTOMERS

Engineered Elastomers & Rubber-to-Metal Bonding

Application-specific HNBR compounds developed with specialized compounding chemists, then molded and bonded in-house to precision-machined metal components. By controlling the metal substrate, surface preparation, bonding, molding, and final inspection, we deliver a fully integrated product from one accountable source.

  • Advanced Series and HyperHeat™ HNBR compounds
  • Engineered for demanding temperature, pressure, and fluid exposure
  • Compression molding and rubber-to-metal bonding
  • Custom colors for identification and product differentiation
  • In-house machining, molding, and final inspection
FABRICATION & REPAIR

Fabrication & Critical Component Repair

Precision welding, dimensional restoration, re-machining, remolding, and final inspection performed under one roof. We recover high-value components through a controlled repair process designed to extend service life, reduce replacement costs, and return dependable equipment to operation.

  • Repair and refurbishment of critical components
  • Precision welding and post-weld machining
  • Dimensional restoration of worn or damaged features
  • Rubber removal, surface preparation, and rebonding
  • Priority support for time-sensitive repairs
  • Documented inspection and full traceability
