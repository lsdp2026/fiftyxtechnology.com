Lone Star Downhole Products, LLC Changes Its Name to Fifty X Technology, LLC

Cypress, Texas precision manufacturer adopts a name that reflects its full scope: CNC machining, engineered elastomers, robotics-enabled production, and fabrication for oil and gas, defense, and industrial customers. Same company, same ownership, same team; only the name changed.

CYPRESS, Texas — September 21, 2026 — Lone Star Downhole Products, LLC today announced that it has changed its legal name to Fifty X Technology, LLC. The Certificate of Amendment was filed with the Texas Secretary of State on September 16, 2026. The company's ownership, leadership, employees, federal employer identification number (EIN), CAGE code, address, telephone number, and banking relationships are unchanged. An assumed name filing preserves "Lone Star Downhole Products" for transition purposes.

Founded in Cypress in 2017 with a single molding press and a proprietary HNBR compound program, the company built its reputation manufacturing MWD and directional drilling components and rubber centralizers for the oilfield. It brought CNC machining in-house in 2019, secured its CAGE code and began federal contracting in 2020, expanded into multi-axis turning and milling in 2022, moved to a two-building campus in 2024, deployed collaborative robots for CNC machine tending in 2025, and in 2026 added enhanced sub-spindle machining and AI-enabled production systems.

"The old name described what we made in 2017. The new one describes what we build today. Nothing about how we serve our MWD and directional drilling customers changes. They will see a new logo on the box and the same people behind it." Ryan Dill, Founder & CEO, Fifty X Technology, LLC

What changes for customers and suppliers

  • Quotes, packing lists, certificates of conformance, and invoices now carry the Fifty X Technology name and logo.
  • Email addresses move to @fiftyxtechnology.com. Former addresses continue to forward during the transition.
  • The company website is now fiftyxtechnology.com. The former domain redirects.
  • Payments should be made payable to Fifty X Technology, LLC. Payments to the former name continue to be accepted during the transition. An updated W-9 is available on request.

What does not change

  • Ownership, management, and the team.
  • EIN, CAGE code (8JPP2), and SAM Unique Entity ID (DP8BSDC81GC3).
  • Part numbers, pricing, contacts, and lead times on all open and future orders.
  • Address, telephone number, and banking and remittance information. Any request to change payment details should be verified by telephone at 713-300-8303 before acting.
  • The company's MWD component and rubber centralizer programs, including its remold program.

About Fifty X Technology

Fifty X Technology, LLC (formerly Lone Star Downhole Products, LLC) is a vertically integrated precision manufacturer in Cypress, Texas. The company combines multi-axis and sub-spindle CNC machining, in-house compression molding with proprietary HNBR compounds, rubber-to-metal bonding, fabrication, and robotic machine tending to produce components for oil and gas, defense, and industrial customers. Founded in 2017, the company holds CAGE code 8JPP2, is SAM registered and JCP certified, and operates an ISO 9001:2015 compliant quality management system. Learn more at fiftyxtechnology.com.
